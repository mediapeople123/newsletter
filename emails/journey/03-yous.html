Jesus Goes Viral: 316 youth shared their faith online.

Hey {$name}!

In my last email I shared what happened in Nashville. Today I want to show you what God did back home in Italy.

On October 31st, Halloween night, we led a two-hour activation at Yous Conference 2026, a national youth event hosted by Sabaoth Church in Milan, with 850 young people from over 80 churches.

We introduced “Jesus Goes Viral”, a challenge inviting young people to create and share content about their faith on their own social media. We gave them simple formats and examples, and two hours later the videos went up together.

The results

316 of the 646 young people we mobilised, almost one in two, shared their faith publicly online, many for the very first time.

Those posts reached over 684,000 views in the first 24 hours, and about 79% of the views on the event’s reels came from people who don’t follow the account.

Watch some of the videos

 

Our vision is to see a generation of digital pioneers equipped and activated to reach their generation with the Gospel.

But our heart doesn’t stop at reaching people online. We want those who encounter the Gospel to have an opportunity to respond, connect with someone and ultimately become part of a local church where they can grow as disciples of Jesus.
