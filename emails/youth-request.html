What it looks like

We run media missions workshops at youth events. Young people learn how to share their faith online, then take on simple challenges: film, share, and invite their friends to take a next step with Jesus. Many of them post about Jesus for the very first time.

What happened at Yous Conference

At our latest event in Italy, 850 young people from over 80 churches gathered, and 646 of them took part in the digital outreach. 316 of them, almost one in two, shared their faith publicly online, reaching over 684,000 views in the first 24 hours.
