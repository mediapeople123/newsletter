My story

I got the mission bug.

In 2012 I went on my first mission trip, travelling around Italy sharing the Gospel on the streets. There was no way back: I got the mission bug.

Since then I have studied film in England, led media for Jesus Revolution in Norway, and filmed what God was doing at our events. In 2025, the content from one single event reached over 10 million views.

But one question would not leave me alone: what happens to all those people after the video ends?

Why now

Then the doors opened.

This summer I was invited into Catalyze Lab, a six-month global accelerator run by OneHope and Missional Labs. In September I flew to Nashville and found myself in rooms with coaches and leaders I never imagined I would meet. Today they are walking with me into what comes next.

What comes next? I will tell you in my first email.

Lately

Where I have been lately.

Over the past six months, God has been using one of the projects to activate young people at youth events, empowering them to share their faith on social media — many for the very first time.

On October 31st, over 300 young people shared their faith on their own profiles, generating more than 650,000 views in less than 24 hours!
