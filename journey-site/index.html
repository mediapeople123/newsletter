My story

Missions changed my life.

I was 16 when I joined my first mission trip, sharing the Gospel on the streets of Italy. I was terrified, but someone believed in me, handed me a mic and encouraged me to share my testimony. It changed the course of my life.

After film school, I became Media Director for Jesus Revolution. I travelled the world, saw hundreds of people saved and healed, and helped young people share their faith online.

Today, I’m 30, and the online world is changing faster than ever. To reach this generation, we need to rethink evangelism. I believe it starts by releasing a new wave of digital evangelists, the young people.
