The Journey · No. 01

God’s confirmation in Nashville.

September 2026 · Catalyze Lab, Nashville, USA

       

Hey {$name}!

On September 16th, I was accepted into Catalyze Lab’s accelerator programme, supporting initiatives bringing the Gospel to the next generation.

It was an incredible experience and a real confirmation of the direction I’ve been carrying in my heart for years.

I was one of just 11 people selected from 76 applicants, and the only representative from Europe.

Luca pitching to the room at Catalyze Lab

The room was filled with people who have been shaping digital evangelism for years, including teams connected to He Gets Us, YouVersion and The Chosen.

I had the opportunity to pitch one of the initiatives I’ve been developing and receive some incredible mentorship, including a one-on-one, 40-minute mentoring conversation with Rob Hoskins.

 

Hearing him say, “Luca, what you are trying to solve, we are also trying to solve. Let’s do it together” was an incredible moment.

This marked the beginning of a six-month collaboration, during which I’ll continue receiving guidance and mentorship from Catalyze Lab as I develop this work further.

Luca speaking at Catalyze Lab · Meeting other founders · The Catalyze Lab programme booklet

More than anything, I genuinely feel that God has been opening these doors and confirming the direction ahead.

I’m excited to see what comes next and to keep you updated as the journey unfolds.

Thank you for being part of it!

Helping people move from an online encounter with Jesus to real discipleship in a local church.
