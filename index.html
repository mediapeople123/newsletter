Chapter 3

The dream

Building digital solutions to bring the gospel to the masses, and to help digital creators take people from an online encounter to physical discipleship.

Simple tools for ministries and creators: capture real testimonies at events, share them far and wide, and when someone responds, connect them with a real believer from a local church near them. Digital should strengthen the local church, not replace it.

Chapter 4

The opportunity: Catalyze Lab

This summer the project was accepted into Catalyze Lab, a six-month global startup accelerator powered by a partnership between OneHope and Missional Labs. In September I flew to Nashville to pitch it, and met coaches and leaders who are now helping me shape it.

Chapter 5

The last event: Yous Conference

From 29 October to 1 November, 650 young people gathered in Italy, and more than 300 of them stepped into digital evangelism for the first time.
