The room was filled with people who have been shaping digital evangelism for years: the teams behind He Gets Us, YouVersion and [OTHER NAMES].

That's the vision I'm carrying forward.

More than anything, I genuinely feel that God has been opening these doors and confirming the direction ahead. I'm excited to see what comes next.
