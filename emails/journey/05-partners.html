As I sense the Lord leading me to dedicate more time to digital evangelism, I’m reminded of Nehemiah. He was called to rebuild the walls of Jerusalem, but he didn’t do it alone. He gathered people around him who shared the vision and were willing to build together.

As we work to build bridges from digital spaces to local churches, I’d love to invite you to become part of a closer circle of people who stand alongside one another and help build this vision.

You can become a mission partner in three different ways

PRAY

 Stand with me in prayer for the work, the people we’re reaching and the opportunities ahead.

CONNECT

 Introduce me to pastors, leaders, churches and organisations. Help open doors and create new opportunities.

GIVE

 Partner financially through monthly support or one-time gifts.
 

As a mission partner, you’ll receive more specific updates about the projects I’m working on, personal prayer requests, and details about particular needs and initiatives you could help make possible.

If you sense the Lord stirring your heart to get involved in one of these ways, I’d love to have a conversation. I can share more about the vision, hear what’s on your heart, and explore what partnering together could look like.

📅  Book a video call

Feel free to choose a time that suits you best, and let’s have a video call and talk about it.
