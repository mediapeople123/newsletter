Why I am building this

Then the screen goes dark.

For years I filmed and shared what God was doing at our events. In 2025, the content from one single event reached over 10 million views. Millions of people saw Jesus on their phones.

But almost none of them were ever connected to a church. A video can start a conversation with God, and then the screen goes dark. Nobody was there for the next step.

What I am building

It is already happening.

Simple tools that help ministries and creators take people from an online encounter to physical discipleship.

We capture real testimonies at events and share them far and wide. When someone responds, they get connected with a real believer from a local church near them. Digital should strengthen the local church, not replace it.

Tested at events. Yous Conference was the latest.

We have been running it at events this year. At the latest, Yous Conference in Italy from 29 October to 1 November, 650 young people gathered and more than 300 of them stepped into digital evangelism for the first time. We gave them challenges: film, share, and invite their friends to take a next step with Jesus.
