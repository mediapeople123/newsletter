So, what does all of this actually look like in practice?

Right now, I’m working across three areas: creating content that points people to Jesus, equipping young people to share their faith online, and developing new ways to help churches activate their youth for digital evangelism.

At the same time, I’m making some changes to create more space for this calling, stepping back from some client projects and dedicating more time to developing the vision, especially during these next few months in the Catalyze Lab accelerator.

I share more about what I’m working on and what this season looks like in this short video.

But there’s something else I want to invite you into.

I don’t believe we’re meant to build alone. Just like Nehemiah, who gathered people to rebuild the walls of Jerusalem, I believe God brings people together to build something bigger than themselves.

I’d love to invite you to become a partner in this mission.

There are three ways you can stand alongside this vision

PRAY: Stand with me in prayer for the work, the people we’re reaching and the opportunities ahead.

CONNECT: Introduce me to pastors, leaders, churches and organisations. Help open doors and create opportunities.

GIVE: Partner financially through monthly support or one-time gifts towards specific projects and initiatives.

What does becoming a partner mean?

It means becoming part of a closer circle of people who are actively standing alongside this mission.

As a partner, you’ll receive more specific updates about the projects we’re developing, personal prayer requests, opportunities to make connections, and details about specific financial needs and initiatives you can help make possible.

You’ll have a closer look at what’s happening behind the scenes, celebrate the fruit, hear about the challenges, and have opportunities to play an active part as the vision develops.

More than simply following the journey, you’ll be helping build it.

If this resonates with you and you’d like to explore becoming a partner, I’d love to have a conversation. We can share more about the vision, get to know each other better and see what partnering together could look like.

Thank you for following along and being part of this journey. I’m genuinely excited about what lies ahead, and I’d love to have you alongside me.
