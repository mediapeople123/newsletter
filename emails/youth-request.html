What it looks like

We run media missions workshops at youth events, helping young people discover practical ways to share their faith online.

Through simple challenges, they create and share content about Jesus, invite their friends to take a next step, and explore how their social media can become a tool for evangelism.

For many, it’s the first time they’ve ever publicly shared their faith online.

What happened at Yous Conference

At our latest event in Italy, 850 young people from over 80 churches gathered. 646 took part in the digital outreach, and 316 of them publicly shared their faith online, reaching over 684,000 views in the first 24 hours.
